I made a lot of progress today and I'm now almost done with my portfolio website. It feels satisfying to finally see how the website looks in reality and how it actually works.

I once created a blog website using Wix, where templates were already provided. I used that site today as part of my layout inspiration. Today felt like a really long day because I spent most of my time focused on working on the website. Even though it was tiring, I enjoyed the process, which made the work feel easier and more fulfilling.

Hobbies & Interests

Scenery

Being outdoors and taking in breathtaking views always lifts my spirits. I find myself captivated by everything that meets my gaze—the towering majesty of a mountain, the endless stretch of a beach, or the quiet serenity of a grassy field. Each scene awakens a unique emotion, telling its own story and capturing a fleeting moment in time. In these sights, I discover calm, wonder, and a deep connection to the world around me. By capturing these moments, I can preserve the feelings they evoke, allowing me to revisit them anytime and relive the simple joy of being fully present in the beauty of nature.

Games

I enjoy playing games to relax and have some fun. Games are meant to be enjoyable, though sometimes they can affect my mood. Online games, in particular, have given me the chance to meet and interact with people from all over the internet. My experiences in these games have made me appreciate teamwork, strategy, and even the small victories, while also giving me a space to unwind and enjoy myself.

Memory

I love capturing and collecting moments from my life, whether they come from big milestones or small, everyday events. Through photos, turn these fleeting moments into memories I can keep and look back on whenever I want. Each strip in my photo album holds a different expression, mood, and story, reminding me of the people I was with and the feelings I shared at that time. By preserving these simple yet meaningful moments, I create a collection of memories that I cherish and revisit whenever I feel nostalgic.
