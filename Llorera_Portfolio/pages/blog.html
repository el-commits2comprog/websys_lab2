Interest Blog

Sceneries

I like to take pictures of views that mesmerize me and of things I don’t want to just see once, so I capture them. It’s a way of holding onto those moments, and somehow it makes me feel like I’m still there, enjoying the view all over again. Each photo reminds me of the calm, the beauty, or the feeling that first made me stop and admire what I was seeing, letting me relive that little moment whenever I look back.

Games

I enjoy playing games to relax and have some fun. Games are meant to be enjoyable, though sometimes they can affect my mood. Online games, in particular, have given me the chance to meet and interact with people from all over the internet. My experiences in these games have made me appreciate teamwork, strategy, and even the small victories, while also giving me a space to unwind and enjoy myself.

Memories

I love capturing and collecting moments from my life, whether they come from big milestones or small, everyday events. Through photos, turn these fleeting moments into memories I can keep and look back on whenever I want. Each strip in my photo album holds a different expression, mood, and story, reminding me of the people I was with and the feelings I shared at that time. By preserving these simple yet meaningful moments, I create a collection of memories that I cherish and revisit whenever I feel nostalgic.
