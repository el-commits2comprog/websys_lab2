Stories That Moved Me

I don't just watch for entertainment. I watch to feel, to understand, and to find pieces of myself in characters I've never met. A good story can change how you see everything. There are a lot more of watches that are my favorite but I feel like these are the ones that I would talk about for hours if given the chance. Each one has a special place in my heart and has taught me something about life, love, or myself. Plus i might overload this page with just my watches alone.

A Note on My Sketches Most of my early sketches and drawings were never documented — loose papers, margins of notebooks, backs of receipts. So many of them are lost now, and honestly, I've made peace with that. I have always had the ability to draw, but as time went on, I lost the passion to do so.

Sound as a Second Language

I don't just listen — I feel. A good album can carry a whole season of memories. Some of these I've played on repeat so many times they've become inseparable from who I am now. I have a lot more of favorite songs that i'll remain to gatekeep as it feels so personal that it speaks my soul :p

What I Listen For

I gravitate toward music that makes me feel something — raw lyrics, unexpected chord changes, a voice that sounds like it's lived something. Genre doesn't matter as much as honesty does.

When I Listen

Mostly while drawing, writing, or walking at night. Music and movement are the two things that quiet the noise in my head better than anything else I've found.

Nature & The Outdoors

Being outdoors grounds me. Whether it's a mountain trail, a city park, or tending to my windowsill garden — nature reminds me of what's real and what truly matters.

The World Looks Different When You Step Outside

There's something about being outside that reminds me I'm alive. A long walk, the city lights blurring in the distance, a sunset bleeding into the sky — these are the moments that quiet everything inside me and remind me that life exists far beyond the four walls of my bedroom.

My Favorite Therapy Has No Appointment

I don't need much — just open air and somewhere to walk. The outside world has a way of putting things into perspective that nothing else quite can. It's where I go when my thoughts get too heavy and where I come back from feeling like myself again.

Sunsets as a Reset

Every sunset feels like the sky's way of saying the day is done and that's enough. I chase them often — from rooftops, street corners, anywhere the horizon is wide enough to hold all that color.

City Lights & Quiet Walks

There's a certain peace in walking through a city at night, lights glowing, the world still moving. It makes me feel small in the best way — like I'm part of something much bigger than whatever's been weighing on me.

Books and Literary Works

Reading is the quietest adventure. It's where I find the most clarity, the most empathy, and the most courage. My shelves are a map of who I am.

My Year of Rest and Relaxation

Ottesa Moshfegh

I found myself in this book more than I'd like to admit — the quiet desire to disappear for a while and come back softer.

Letters to Milena

Franz Kafka

Kafka writes the way I feel things — too deeply, too honestly. This book made me feel less alone in loving someone through words.

The Midnight Library

Matt Haig

A reminder that the life you're living is still worth choosing. I come back to this one whenever I need to be talked down from my own what-ifs.

Conversations on Love

Natasha Lunn

Love as something you study, question, and grow into. This book put language to things I'd only ever felt quietly.

The Myth of Sisyphus

Albert Camus

Camus asked the hardest question and answered it with defiance. This essay taught me to keep going — not despite the absurdity, but because of it.

Kafka on the Shore

Haruki Murakami

Murakami writes worlds that feel like walking through a dream you can't quite shake. This one stayed with me the way a good walk does — long after it's over.
