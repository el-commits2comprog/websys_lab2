Nature & The Outdoors

Being outdoors grounds me. Whether it's a mountain trail, a city park, or tending to my windowsill garden — nature reminds me of what's real and what truly matters.

How I've Grown Into the Person I Am Now

Growing up near the coast, I developed an early love for tidal pools and sea glass. Now I carry that love wherever I go — in the way I slow down on a forest path or the joy I feel spotting a new bird species.

Books and Literary Works

Reading is the quietest adventure. It's where I find the most clarity, the most empathy, and the most courage. My shelves are a map of who I am.

A meditation on nature and what it means to truly see the world around us.

The Overstory

Richard Powers

An epic novel about trees, humans, and interconnectedness that shifted my perspective.

A Field Guide to Getting Lost

Rebecca Solnit

Essays on wandering, loss, and the beautiful uncertainty of life. My most annotated book.

The Creative Habit

A practical guide to building a creative practice that shaped how I approach art daily.
