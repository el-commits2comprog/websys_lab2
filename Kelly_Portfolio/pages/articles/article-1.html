Some Seasons Break You. This One Built Me.

February 2026 · Self · 4 min read

Academics Self Writing
February 20, 2026
Academics
4 minutes

I used to be the person who had the "just go with the flow" mindset. For a while, it seemed okay — no expectations, no structure. But as I progressed in life and was growing into new experiences, I realized just how dangerous this mindset could be. It reveals how one does not have a definite plan and idea of what they actually want in life.

A Mindset That Cost Me My First Choice

This was the mindset I was on when I took the entrance exam for the university I was aiming for. I got a high score, but it was not enough for my first choice, which was BS Nursing. I was not able to secure a slot, hence I applied when the CSIT Department opened. BSIT was actually a spontaneous second choice, it was one that my friend just introduced me to because I was hesitant to pick Architecture.

Fear Over Opportunity

Most of my life before, I never tried things outside of my comfort zone. I may have excelled academically, but I never tried to explore outside of it — even when there were opportunities that were handed to me. Fear was greater than my desire to exceed my limitations.

When I got accepted in BSIT, I really felt like it was the turning point of my life. I remember asking God that if this was not for me, I was willing to take a gap year — and if this were for me, I'd gladly accept it. It felt like every change that I've encountered in my life has led to this moment, I just had never known.

The Hardest Year

My first year was full of burnouts and doubts about whether I was really on the right path. I admit that I had a hard time adjusting and coping with a new environment, especially in one where I do not have enough previous knowledge on unlike the others. I felt like I lost the passion and the spark I once had so brightly felt far beyond my reach.

But just today, when all our grades have been released on our university's student portal, I calculated my GWA and saw that I was part of the Dean's List. It felt surreal, I double checked it tons of times to make sure I was not hallucinating and did not miscalculate. But it was real, I actually did it.

Receiving this confirmation on my screen felt like a reminder that my spark is still within me — it was just a quiet ember.

I was beginning to love the course that I was once unsure about. I am more motivated, disciplined, and passionate about the things I want to do. Because now, I know what I want and I have to have it — or at least try.

Building Something Sure

I am in the season of my life where I want to build foundations. I may not be entirely sure of how things may go, but I'll keep on trying anyway. That will be my mindset every time. I just have to keep on showing up — especially when it's hard.

For once in my life, I felt like I was building something I am sure of. And just how amazing it is to know what you want and strive towards it.
