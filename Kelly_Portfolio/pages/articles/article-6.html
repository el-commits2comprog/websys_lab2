Notes on Gratitude

November 2023 · Reflection · 6 min read

Reflection Gratitude Mental Health
November 23, 2023
Reflection
6 minutes

I used to think gratitude was something you practiced on designated days—Thanksgiving, birthdays, the occasional moment of exceptional fortune. But I've learned that gratitude is not an emotion to wait for; it's a practice to cultivate daily.

The Small Things Matter Most

It's not always about the big moments. Yes, I'm grateful for my dreams coming true, for the people I love, for opportunities I've been given. But I've also started noticing smaller things: the exact temperature of my morning coffee, the way afternoon light hits my studio wall, a text from a friend I hadn't heard from in months.

These micro-moments of gratitude add up. When you start noticing them, your day becomes richer. The world becomes kinder, not because it changed, but because you're seeing it differently.

Gratitude as Resilience

During difficult times, gratitude doesn't erase the pain—but it provides a foundation. When I'm struggling, I write three things I'm grateful for. It's not toxic positivity or bypassing real struggles. It's acknowledging that light and dark coexist, and by recognizing the light, we're better equipped to handle the darkness.

"Gratitude is not only the greatest of virtues, but the parent of all others." — Marcus Tullius Cicero

Some of my most transformative periods came after the most difficult ones. It wasn't until I practiced gratitude—even gratitude for the lessons learned—that I could fully move forward.

Building a Gratitude Practice

I keep a gratitude journal. Not a fancy one—just a simple notebook where I jot down 3-5 things daily. Some entries are profound; others are wonderfully mundane. Both matter.

I also practice gratitude out loud. I tell the people I love specifically what I appreciate about them. I thank the barista, the bus driver, the librarian. Gratitude shared becomes exponential.

Closing Thoughts

As we move into a new season, I'm inviting you to join me in this practice. Not as a resolution or obligation, but as an offering to yourself. Watch how gratitude shifts your perspective. Notice how it changes your days, your relationships, your capacity for joy.

The world is waiting to be appreciated. Are you ready?
