Writing Was Always My Safest Place

I didn't start out thinking of myself as a writer. I was just someone who felt too much and needed somewhere to put it all. So I wrote. First in journals nobody would ever read, then in captions, then in longer pieces that surprised even me with how much they said. Somewhere along the way, the writing stopped being an outlet and started being a voice — mine, finally, in a form I could stand behind.

"I write because some feelings are too big to just carry around quietly."

This blog is a little bit of everything I am. It's the girl who stands in golden hour light and forgets to be anywhere else. It's the overthinker, the quiet observer, the one who notices things and can't help but write them down.
