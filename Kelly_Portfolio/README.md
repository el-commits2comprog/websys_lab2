## Kelly Portfolio — *love, ely*

A personal portfolio and blog space by Lovely Joy Kelly, a 2nd year BS Information Technology student at Bicol University.

### Pages
- **Home** — Introduction, timeline, achievements, interests, and the people I lives for
- **Daily Blogs** — A collection of personal essays and reflections
- **More About Lovely** — Deep dive into my favorite films, music, books, and the outdoors

### Blog Articles
1. *Some Seasons Break You. This One Built Me.* — On making the Dean's List and finding my spark
2. *All Is Well — and This Day Proved It* — Watching *3 Idiots* with friends, cooking together, and what the film meant
3. *Familiar Fear, Familiar Strength* — On change as the only constant, and always coming home to yourself
4. *I Can't Control the Tide and I'm Learning to Be Okay With That* — On having big emotions and learning to befriend them
5. *It Ends So It Can Begin Again* — On temporary things, unplanned goodbyes, and beginning again
6. *Moving Forward When Everything Feels Too Heavy* — On getting unstuck, walking it out, and showing up every day
7. *Writing Was Always My Safest Place* — An introduction to the blog and the power of putting words to feeling

### Tech Stack
- HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts — Playfair Display & DM Sans
- No frameworks — fully hand-coded

## How to Run Locally

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/el-commits2comprog/websys_lab2.git
   ```
2. Open either portfolio's `index.html` directly in your browser, or use a local server:
   ```bash
   cd Kelly_Portfolio
   npx serve .
   ```
